逐句跟读卡

1中文原句

尊老是中华民族的传统美德，深深植根于中国人的思想和行为中。

1英文参考译文

Respect for the elderly is a traditional virtue of the Chinese nation, deeply rooted in the thoughts and behaviour of the Chinese people.

重点短语
  • 传统美德 — a traditional virtue
  • 深深植根于 — deeply rooted in / deeply embedded in
  • 思想和行为 — thoughts and behaviour
译点：无主句补主语 "Respect for the elderly"；"深深植根于" 首选 "deeply rooted in"，书面可换 "deeply embedded in"。
2中文原句

尊老这一美德深深植根于中国人的思想和行为中。

2英文参考译文

This virtue is deeply rooted in the thoughts and behaviour of the Chinese people.

重点短语
  • 尊老这一美德 — this virtue of respecting the elderly
  • 深深植根于 — deeply rooted in
译点："这一美德" 的 "这一" 是呼应性重复，不必逐字译 "this virtue" 即达意；中文重前后呼应、英文重简洁。
3中文原句

尊老是中华民族的传统美德。

3英文参考译文

Respect for the elderly is a traditional virtue of the Chinese nation.

重点短语
  • 中华民族 — the Chinese nation
  • 传统美德 — a traditional virtue
译点："中华民族" 译 "the Chinese nation" 较 "the Chinese people" 更庄重，契合书面语域。
4中文原句

尊老是人们普遍遵守的行为规范和社会准则。

4英文参考译文

Respect for the elderly is a behavioural norm and social standard universally observed by people.

重点短语
  • 行为规范和社会准则 — behavioural norm and social standard / social norms
  • 普遍遵守 — universally observed / generally followed
译点："行为规范和社会准则" 可合并简化为 "social norms"；"普遍遵守" 用 "universally observed / generally followed" 更地道。
5中文原句

中国政府也在不断完善养老保障体系，提高养老金水平。

5英文参考译文

The Chinese government is also improving the pension system and raising old-age pension levels.

重点短语
  • 养老保障体系 — pension system / old-age insurance system
  • 养老金水平 — old-age pension levels
  • 不断完善 — constantly improving / refining
译点："养老保障体系" 官方常作 "pension system" 或 "old-age insurance system"；"不断完善" 用 "constantly refining" 更有推进感。
6中文原句

确保老年人安享晚年。

6英文参考译文

Ensure that the elderly enjoy a happy old age.

重点短语
  • 确保 — ensure
  • 老年人 — the elderly
  • 安享晚年 — enjoy a happy old age（官方固定译法）
译点：无主句可直接以 "Ensure" 起祈使句；"安享晚年" 官方固定译 "enjoy a happy old age"。
